Thomas Rupsis

Trumpet player and music educator based in Cedar Park, Texas. Received a Bachelor of Music in Music Education from Texas Tech University (2026).

About Thomas

Educational Background

Originally from Billings, Montana, Thomas Rupsis attended Billings West High School, where he was named to the Montana All-State Band and Orchestra for four consecutive years, as well as the NAfME All-Northwest Band/Orchestra.

He moved to Texas to earn his Bachelor of Music in Music Education at Texas Tech University. His primary teachers have been Dr. Julia Bell and Dr. Kevin Whalen. While at TTU, he has earned places on both the President's List and Dean's List, and has served as President of the Raider Trumpet Guild and Ambassador for the Talkington College of Visual & Performing Arts.

Performance Experience

National Trumpet Competition, 2026

At Texas Tech University, Thomas has performed as a member of the school's top ensembles, including the Symphonic Wind Ensemble, University Symphony Orchestra, and the Jazz Orchestra. He was a member of the 2026 FOOSA Philharmonic Orchestra Festival in Fresno, California, and has traveled to Mexico City to perform in the 2025 EuroJazz festival. He is an active participant in the National Trumpet Competition, placing 1st in the 2024 large ensemble division. He advanced to semifinals in the 2025 large ensemble division, quarterfinals in the 2023 and 2026 large ensemble divisions, and quarterfinals in the 2026 undergraduate solo division. In addition to various chamber brass and mixed ensembles, he was a member of the Hub City Brass Quintet, the premiere undergraduate brass quintet at Texas Tech University.

Teaching Experience

As an educator, Thomas has taught freelance private trumpet lessons since 2019, working with brass students ranging from beginning middle schoolers to advanced high school players preparing for region and state auditions. He is currently student teaching with the concert and marching band programs at Vandegrift High School and at Canyon Ridge Middle School in Leander ISD.

Marching Experience

An avid advocate for the marching arts, Thomas was a lead trumpet player for the Boston Crusaders Drum and Bugle Corps in 2024 and 2025. In 2024, he earned a silver medal and the Boston “Excellence in Brass” award. In 2025, he earned a gold medal and received the Jim Ott High Brass award. Thomas will join the Madison Scouts for their 2027 season as a trumpet instructor and member of the brass educational staff.

Private Studio

Trumpet Lessons

In-person and virtual individualized trumpet instruction focusing on developing a solid fundamental foundation, musicality, and audition preparation. Competitive rates with flexible availability. Find the contact form below to learn more!

Teaching Philosophy

Playing the trumpet should feel natural and musical. Lessons are individually personalized to help the player achieve their own goals, while also refining fundamentals. Whether preparing for middle school all-region auditions or competitive TMEA All-State and college entrance trials, students build sustainable technique that eliminates unnecessary tension.

Middle School

Foundation & All-Region

Ideal for 6th–8th graders developing a rich sound, establishing good practice habits, reading skills, tone quality, and middle school all-region audition music.

High School

All-State & College Prep

Preparation for TMEA All-State etudes, UIL Solo & Ensemble contest, ensemble trumpet playing, and college auditions.

Clinics & Tech

Marching & Masterclasses

Sectionals and visual/brass clinics for high school marching programs, drum corps audition prep, and brass technique.
